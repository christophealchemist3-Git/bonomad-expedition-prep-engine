# BonNomad – Expedition Prep Engine

**AI-powered expedition planning for off-road and overland travel.**

BonNomad is a functional prototype designed to explore how AI can simplify the preparation of off-road expeditions and overland journeys by combining vehicle constraints, itinerary planning and contextual recommendations.

The project explores a product-oriented approach to AI, turning complex travel requirements into a structured and personalized planning experience.

## The Challenge

Preparing an off-road expedition involves more than selecting a destination. Travellers must consider vehicle capabilities, terrain, autonomy, route constraints, logistics and preparation requirements.

BonNomad explores how an AI-assisted platform can bring these elements together in a more coherent and user-friendly experience.

## Key Capabilities

- **Personalized expedition planning** based on user requirements and vehicle characteristics.
- **Itinerary exploration** for off-road and overland travel.
- **Contextual recommendations** to support trip preparation.
- **Expedition readiness** through structured preparation and checklists.
- **AI-assisted product experience** designed around practical user needs.
